========================================================================
                 AmnesiaIDE — PERSISTENT STORAGE HANDBOOK
========================================================================

Amnesia OS runs entirely in RAM (Amnesic Mode). All system files, configurations,
and local modifications in RAM are lost forever upon reboot or shutdown.

To allow you to develop programs and keep your files across reboots, Amnesia OS 
supports Physical Disk Persistence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW PERSISTENCE WORKS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When you click [ SAVE & BUILD ] inside the IDE:
  - The OS mounts the second partition of your virtual disk (/dev/vda2)
    to the permanent mount point: /persist
  - Your projects are copied into /persist/AmnesiaIDE/
  - The IDE compiles your main.c using the physical toolchain.
  - The binary is compiled to /persist/AmnesiaIDE/main.
  - Files are safely synced to the hardware disk, ensuring that they 
    survive amnesic memory purges!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. COMPILED BINARY EXECUTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
To run your compiled persistent C program, open the Terminal [>_] and run:
  $ /persist/AmnesiaIDE/main

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MANUAL DISK MOUNTING
------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you want to manually interact with your persistent files in the shell:
  $ mount /dev/vda2 /persist
  $ cd /persist/AmnesiaIDE/
  $ ls -la
